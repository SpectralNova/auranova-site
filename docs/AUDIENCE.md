# Audience profile

*v3 · 2026-10-19 · CP1 feedback ✅: owners of **small and mid-sized businesses** (no big corporate clients) who want **fast, affordable, reliable** websites **and advice**. Mexico only; the US is on hold (Q4). The "words they use" are hypotheses to check on the first calls.*

## Primary: the owner who decides

Owner or manager of a **small or mid-sized business** in **Monterrey / Mexico** (Q3). Real examples so far: an industrial distributor (FERREMAG-type) and an education/services business (LOGOS-type).

- **Non-technical.** Judges by what they see on their phone and by how clearly things are explained.
- **Phone-first and WhatsApp-native.** Often opens our link from a WhatsApp chat or a Google search on mobile.
- **Decides alone or with a partner.** No procurement department; trust in the person matters.
- **Compares 2–3 quotes.** Needs to understand quickly what they get for the money.

## What they're trying to get done

- Get inquiries or quote requests from the website, not just "have a page".
- Look as professional online as they are in person.
- Regain control: their own domain, accounts and files.
- Fix a slow or broken WordPress site, or replace manual work with a portal (LOGOS-type).

## Pains (speak to these, in their words)

| Pain | Where the site answers it |

## Words they probably use (verify on calls)

"página web", "que me lleguen clientes", "cotización", "que se vea profesional", "que cargue rápido", "que salga en Google", "que yo pueda cambiarle cosas".
We avoid jargon ("CMS", "Core Web Vitals", "SEO técnico") in headlines; when a term is needed, we explain it in one line.

## What they're buying (CP1 ✅)

- **Fast:** a site that loads fast, and a studio that answers fast (1 día hábil).
- **Affordable:** a fixed price that fits a small business's budget, paid in stages. No public prices, but the message is clear.
- **Reliable:** everything in writing, measured quality, and the accounts in their name.
- **Advice:** someone who tells them what they need, and what they don't, before they spend.

## Secondary readers

- **The person who forwards the link:** a manager or assistant who researches and sends options to the owner. Needs scannable pages and a shareable link per service.
- **Referrers:** past clients or contacts recommending AuraNova. Need a page that confirms the recommendation fast (Nosotros, the standard).

## Implications for design and copy

- Mobile-first at 320–390 px; key answers visible without opening menus.
- One verb-led CTA everywhere ("Agenda una llamada"); the business email as the written channel (Q15).
- Every claim specific and checkable; no jargon in headlines.
- Show the method and the terms in writing, since trust is the main barrier. AuraNova speaks as a studio (Q13, Q14).
