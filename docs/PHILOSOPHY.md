## 1. The idea behind the name (proposed concept) 🟡

- **Nova:** a star that suddenly becomes far brighter and is seen from far away. That's the outcome we sell: a business that becomes **visible** online.
- **Aura:** the presence something gives off before anyone reads a word. A website is a business's aura: people judge it in seconds.

Put together: **we make businesses shine online, and the shine is earned, not decorative.** The brightness comes from method:

- a clear purpose for every page;
- everything written down;
- quality you can measure.

That keeps the name tied to the values in §4 instead of being just a pretty word.

The brand already looks like this. The logo is a celestial body with a lime → violet glow. In the design system, the blurred gradient orb and the soft lime glow on the main button are, literally, the aura. That's why the rule is **one glowing element per view**: an aura is focused, not everywhere.

Tagline candidates (es-MX):

1. **"Sitios web que hacen brillar a tu negocio."**
2. "Brillo que se mide." *(pairs with the value "Calidad que se mide")*
3. "Tu negocio, con aura propia."

## 2. Misión 🟡

> **Diseñamos y desarrollamos sitios web con un propósito de negocio claro: rápidos, fáciles de usar, accesibles y medibles. Trabajamos con un proceso transparente en el que ves tu sitio desde el principio y siempre sabes qué sigue, cuánto cuesta y quién es responsable de qué.**

*Intent:* the first sentence is the product (sites that work) and the second is the experience (no surprises). Every value below supports one of the two.

## 4. Valores

Every value has to show up as a behavior a client can check. If it can't be checked, it's a slogan, not a value.

### 4.1 Propósito antes que decoración
> *"Cada página existe para que alguien haga algo."*

- Before any page is designed, it gets a one-paragraph **page brief** with its goal, its audience, and **one primary CTA**.
- The goal is measured (an analytics event on the primary CTA).
- Sources: non-negotiables #1 "It must have a purpose" and #14 "Clear calls to action".

### 4.2 Diseñamos para quien te visita
> *"Tu sitio es para tus clientes. Nuestro gusto, y el tuyo, van después."*

- The audience is defined at kickoff, and design decisions cite who they serve.
- Copy uses the customer's words, not industry jargon.
- Source: non-negotiable #2 "Design for the audience, not the client".

### 4.3 Primero lo ves
> *"Antes de construir a fondo, ves tu sitio real en tu propio teléfono."*

- At checkpoint CP1, the client gets a **design preview built in the real stack**, at a private link, with real content wherever it exists.
- The client approves the look before the full build starts, so nothing is a surprise at launch.
- Source: your own practice ("see first, test later" on LOGOS and FERREMAG).

### 4.4 Todo por escrito
> *"Si no está escrito, no está acordado. Y si está escrito, se cumple."*

- **Anexo A** (scope) lists every deliverable.
- Every change goes through a **written change request**, with its cost and date impact, *before* the work starts.
- Decisions are logged.
- We never invent numbers, testimonials, or client logos.
- Sources: contract §1 and §5; Danbee Shin's video (the services clause as the cure for scope creep).

### 4.5 Calidad que se mide
> *"No decimos 'rápido' ni 'accesible': lo demostramos con números."*

- Every deliverable passes the **Estándar de entrega** (§7) and ships with a short report:
  - Core Web Vitals
  - Lighthouse
  - WCAG 2.2 AA checks
  - a 320 px mobile test
- Sources: the 15 non-negotiables, and the acceptance criteria in your client briefs.

### 4.6 Lo tuyo es tuyo
> *"Cuando terminas de pagar, el sitio, el código, el dominio y los accesos son tuyos."*

- The domain, hosting, and third-party accounts are in the **client's name from day one**.
- Full IP assignment happens on final payment.
- The client gets a **handover kit**: credentials shared through a password manager, documentation, and a training session.
- No lock-in, ever.
- Sources: contract §6; video (IP section).

### 4.7 Respeto al tiempo, en ambos sentidos
> *"Cumplimos horarios y fechas, y pedimos lo mismo."*

- Fixed business hours, and response times written into the contract.
- If approvals arrive late, the schedule moves by the same amount.
- Pausing a project has a restart cost.
- Sources: your hours (✅); video (project-schedule clause).

## 7. Estándar de entrega: the 15 non-negotiables, made measurable

The Kindling article states the principles but gives almost no thresholds. The numbers below are proposed from public standards: Google's Core Web Vitals "good" thresholds and WCAG 2.2 AA. They apply to **every page of every client project**, this site included. Each deliverable ships with this table filled in (pass/fail plus evidence).

| # | Principle | How we meet it (measurable) | Verified with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | It must have a purpose | Every page has a written brief: goal, audience, one primary CTA, and a success metric. | Page brief file per page |
| 2 | Design for the audience, not the client | The audience profile is written at kickoff; every section's brief names who it serves. | CP0 doc |
| 3 | Fast load speeds | Mobile p75: **LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1**. Lighthouse Performance **≥ 90** (mobile). Images in AVIF/WebP with explicit dimensions; woff2 fonts subset, preloaded. | Lighthouse / PageSpeed report |
| 4 | Consistency | Every color, size, spacing, and radius comes from the design-system tokens; no one-off values. | Code review: grep for raw hex / px values outside tokens |
| 5 | Distinctive branding | Logo **top-left on every page**, linked to home; favicon; default OG image. | Visual check of every template |
| 6 | Easy navigation | ≤ 7 top-level items; every page within **3 clicks** of home; current page marked; useful 404; footer repeats the key links. | Sitemap review, click test |
| 7 | White space | Spacing only from the scale; body text **60–75 characters** per line. | Visual / CSS check |
| 8 | Use symmetry | 12-column grid; consistent alignment axes per section. | Grid overlay screenshot |
| 9 | Choose colours wisely | Brand palette only; text contrast **≥ 4.5:1** (large text and UI ≥ 3:1). | Token contrast report |
| 10 | Readable fonts | **≤ 3 families**; body **≥ 16 px**, line-height 1.5–1.7; no light weights for body text. | CSS check |
| 11 | Engaging content | Real, specific copy; no lorem ipsum at launch; no invented stats, testimonials, or logos. | `[[PENDIENTE` grep = 0 in production |
| 12 | No huge text chunks | Paragraphs ≤ ~4 lines on desktop; a heading at least every ~300 words; lists for 3+ parallel items. | Content review |
| 13 | Avoid annoyances | No entry pop-ups, no autoplay with sound, no scroll-jacking, no chat bubbles covering content; carousels never auto-advance without a pause control. | Manual check |
| 14 | Clear calls to action | One primary CTA per view, labeled with a verb; a CTA in the first viewport and at the end of every page. | Visual check |
| 15 | Mobile-friendly | Mobile-first; tested at **320, 375, 768, 1024, 1440 px**; no horizontal scroll; tap targets ≥ 44 px (WCAG 2.2 minimum is 24 px). | Screenshot set |

**Additions beyond the 15** (from your existing client briefs, applied by default):

- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard-only flows work, focus is visible, fields are labeled, and pages work at 200% zoom.
- **Technical SEO:** unique title and description per page; JSON-LD; sitemap; correct robots rules; clean URLs.
- **Privacy:** a privacy notice (*aviso de privacidad*) under Mexico's current personal-data law (LFPDPPP) whenever any personal data is collected; cookieless analytics unless the client needs more.
- **Security:** HTTPS plus HSTS; no secrets or business facts hard-coded; dependencies kept current; spam protection on forms.
- **Handover:** credentials, documentation, and a client-editable surface in Spanish.

## 8. Lo que no hacemos (boundaries)

- No inventamos cifras, reseñas ni logos de clientes.
- No retenemos dominios, accesos ni archivos como rehenes.
- No usamos plantillas, fuentes, fotos ni plugins sin licencia.
- No ponemos pop-ups, autoplay ni trucos que molesten a tus visitantes.
- No tocamos tu sitio en producción sin tu aprobación por escrito.
- No trabajamos fuera del alcance sin una solicitud de cambio aprobada.
- No recomendamos herramientas que no necesitas. *(Real example: on LOGOS you advised against GoHighLevel because it cost $97 USD/month and didn't cover the portal.)*

---

## 9. Voz y tono

- **Clear, direct, warm.** No unnecessary technical terms. When one is needed, explain it in one line.
- Address the client informally with **"tú"**, the norm for Mexican business-casual (Backdoor does this too).
- ✅ **"Nosotros"** (Q14): AuraNova speaks as a studio. Never imply a team size, years or numbers that aren't real.
- **English** (Q4): the same voice in US English: plain, direct, warm, "you". Adapted for US readers, not translated word for word.
- Headlines promise an outcome, not a feature ("Más clientes desde tu sitio" beats "Diseño responsivo").
- Every claim is specific and verifiable.
