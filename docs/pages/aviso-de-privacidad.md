## Outline (light theme)

1. Identidad y domicilio del responsable (Q2).
2. Datos que recabamos: form fields, booking data, minimal technical data. No sensitive data.
3. Finalidades: answer the inquiry and prepare a proposal (primary); anything secondary needs its own opt-out (likely none).
4. Transferencias y encargados: host (Q19), email provider (ADR-003), booking tool (Q16), analytics (Q18, cookieless).
5. Derechos ARCO and revoking consent: procedure, contact channel (Q15), response times per the law.
6. Cookies y tecnologías similares: none if Q18 is cookieless; say so.
7. Cambios al aviso: how they're announced, with a "última actualización" date.
